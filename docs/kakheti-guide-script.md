# Kakheti Beta Guide Script

A friendly script for the guide running the first Ivera Kakheti beta tour.  
Read it before the tour. You do not need to memorise it — adapt it to your own words.

---

## Before the tour starts

**When meeting the tourist / group:**

> "Welcome! Today we are going on the Kakheti Wine and Legends Quest — not just a tour, but a real adventure through Georgia's most beautiful wine region. You will visit sacred monasteries, walk ancient city walls, taste the oldest wine in the world, and sit down for a Georgian feast. And for each experience, you will earn points in the Ivera app."

**Pause, then:**

> "Do you have your phone? Open your browser and go to: ivera-web-app.vercel.app — or I can send you the link right now."

Send the link via WhatsApp or show the URL clearly.

---

## Setting up the Explorer Pass

**When they open the app:**

> "You will see a button that says 'Start Your Journey'. Tap it and create your Explorer Pass — just your name, where you are from, and what you are interested in. It takes 30 seconds and it saves your XP progress across all quests."

**If they ask what XP is:**

> "XP means Experience Points. Every time you complete a mission today, you earn points. At the end, you will see your total score and unlock a special badge — the Wine Connoisseur badge. It is only earned by people who complete this quest."

**Wait until everyone has created their pass before departing.**

---

## At each mission location

### Mission 1 — The Saint's Resting Place (Bodbe Monastery)

**Before entering:**

> "We are about to visit Bodbe Monastery — the resting place of Saint Nino, the woman who brought Christianity to Georgia in 337 AD. She walked here from what is now Turkey, on foot, alone. This is one of the most sacred places in the country. Take a moment before we go in."

**At the tomb:**

> "This is her tomb. Georgians have been coming here for 1,700 years to pray and to remember. Open the Ivera app, find Mission 1 — The Saint's Resting Place — and tap Demo Scan. You have earned 75 XP."

---

### Mission 2 — City of Love Panorama (Sighnaghi Viewpoint)

**When they first see the view:**

> "Look at this. The Alazani Valley stretches all the way to the Greater Caucasus mountains. On a clear day you can see the snow-capped peaks. Sighnaghi has been called the City of Love — this is why."

**After they take it in:**

> "Open Mission 2 in the app and tap Demo Scan. Capture this view if you want — it is one of the most photographed spots in Kakheti. You just earned 100 XP."

---

### Mission 3 — Guardian of the Walls (Sighnaghi City Walls)

**While walking the walls:**

> "These walls were built by King Erekle II in the 1780s to protect Kakheti from Persian and Ottoman raids. There are 23 watchtowers. Can you count how many you can see from here?"

**Let them count, then:**

> "Open Mission 3 and tap Demo Scan. 75 XP. You are now a guardian of Sighnaghi."

---

### Mission 4 — The Amber Wine Ritual (Wine Tasting Hall)

**Before the tasting:**

> "We are about to taste something unique in the world. Georgia invented wine 8,000 years ago. This wine is made in a clay vessel called a qvevri — buried underground, sealed with beeswax, aged for months. It is orange in colour. It is called amber wine. There is nothing like it anywhere else."

**When the wine is poured:**

> "Before you drink — open Mission 4 in the app and tap Demo Scan. Now close your eyes and taste. What do you notice? Dried fruit? Nuts? The soil? Tell me what you taste."

**After tasting:**

> "You just tasted 8,000 years of Georgian winemaking. 125 XP — the most valuable mission of the day."

---

### Mission 5 — Tamada: Master of the Toast (Supra Table)

**When everyone is seated at the supra:**

> "In Georgia, every feast has a tamada — a toast master. The tamada leads the toasts, tells the stories, and connects everyone at the table. Today, you are the tamada."

**Teach them:**

> "The most important word: Gaumarjos. It means 'victory' — but in Georgia it means so much more. It means: may you win at life. Repeat after me: Gau-mar-jos."

**After they say it:**

> "Now open Mission 5 in the app, tap Demo Scan, and deliver your toast to the table. Make it from the heart."

**After the toast:**

> "You have earned 100 XP — and the respect of everyone at this table. Total for today: 475 XP. You are a Wine Connoisseur."

---

## When all missions are complete

> "You have completed the Kakheti Wine and Legends Quest. Check your app — you should see the completion screen with your badge and total XP."

**Show them the feedback button:**

> "There is a green button that says 'Send Feedback via WhatsApp'. Please tap it and send me your honest thoughts — what you loved, what we can improve. It goes directly to Levani who built this tour, and your feedback shapes the next version."

---

## If Demo Scan does not work

**Stay calm and matter-of-fact:**

> "Sometimes the app takes a second. Try tapping the button again. If it still does not work, let me know — we can continue the story and fix the points later."

**If the quest page is not loading:**

> "Try closing the browser tab and opening the link again: ivera-web-app.vercel.app — then go to Tours → Kakheti → Start Quest."

**Never let a technical issue interrupt the story.** The experience is the most important thing. XP can be sorted afterwards.

---

## At the end of the tour

> "Thank you for joining the first beta group of Ivera Travel Quests. You helped us test something new in Georgia. If you loved it — tell your friends. If you have suggestions — send them in the app. Either way, you are part of the story now."

**Exchange WhatsApp contacts if they want to be updated on future quests.**
